## Coverage notes

- **R2 (Data Infrastructure)** is the thinnest authoritatively — NHS material is
  data-quality benchmarking/standards rather than a single "build an AI data feed"
  framework.
- **R4 (Human Oversight Capacity)** has **no dedicated UK/NHS automation-bias
  training product**; it's covered indirectly. If you want a true "training" entry
  here, that's the gap.

## Caveats to record

- ICO AI guidance + AI Data Protection Risk Toolkit are flagged "under review"
  following the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
- DTAC: form v2.0 (Feb 2026) is current; old `transform.england.nhs.uk` URL now
  redirects to `digital.nhs.uk`.
- Buyer's Guide: original NHSX PDF archived; the `digital.nhs.uk` chapters +
  assessment template are the canonical entry point.
